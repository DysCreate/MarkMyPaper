What an account does here

Grading does not require an account on the default setup. The upload route is open, so you can grade a sheet immediately.

Registration creates an entry in the local SQLite database with a hashed password. The API returns a JWT that your browser keeps in local storage. Nothing about your account, your files, or your grading runs leaves your machine.

endpoints used: POST /api/register and POST /api/login
