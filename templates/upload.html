Grade a sheet

Everything runs on this machine. The file is read, transcribed if needed, and scored against your model answers, then the run is saved to local history.

Step 01 / the sheet

PDF, JPG, JPEG, or PNG. Multi-page PDFs are handled page by page.

Step 02 / model answers and weights

The weight is the maximum marks the answer can earn. At 80% similarity or better the full weight is earned; between 70% and 79% the reward scales with similarity; below 70% the answer earns nothing.

Step 03 / options

First OCR run can take a minute: the TrOCR model is downloaded once from Hugging Face, then transcription uses your CPU or GPU.
